Tagalong's Daily Life

Tagalong spends lots of time with her family, even when they're engrossed in other things.

Tagalong lives a fairly simple life. As a housecat, she has all the comforts a cat could ask for, with a nice routine.

Tagalong munching away on some food.

While Tagalong and her brother Creamsicle were free fed for many years, at the suggestion of their vet, they've been on a meal schedule for around three years. Tagalong, for one, is always excited for dinnertime.

To learn more about free feeding, and whether it may be right for your cat, the linked article is a good starting point, though ultimately the decision on whether or not to free feed should be discussed with a vet. Free Feeding Cats

Tagalong acting cute.

Tagalong doesn't just spend all day munching. She also enjoys other activities, such as playing, or being brushed. For a furry cat such as Tagalong, brushing is essential.

Tagalong, sleeping soundly, at quite the angle.

Of course, there's also sleeping.
