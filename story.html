Tagalong's Story

Tagalong loves Christmas, as every year she is spoiled with presents.

Tagalong was adopted by the Sinkwitz family on January 26, 2020 from Foothills Animal Shelter in Scottsdale, Arizona. Before that, her story is somewhat of a mystery. Before she was first adopted, she was known as Oki, and she was found in Casa Grande. The shelter said she was eight years old, though several visits to the vet confirmed she was closer to five years old. Little is known about Tagalong's life in Casa Grande, but nowadays she's very happy in Scottsdale with her mom, dad, three human kids, and cat brother.

Tagalong snuggling with her dad.

Tagalong, as one might expect from her name, has some affiliation with the Girl Scouts. When she was first adopted, it was cookie season for the two older kids, and wanting to carry on the Girl Scout spirit, Oki became Tagalong. The name was apt, as over the years, Tagalong has proved herself to love 'tagging along', and loves to hang out with her family, no matter what they may be up to.

Tagalong isn't immune to mischief. One of her favorite activities is hopping up on the dryer.

Tagalong, now aged 11, still provides her family joy and laughter in their day to day lives, and hopefully will for many years to come.
