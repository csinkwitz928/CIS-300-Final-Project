Life in a Multi-Cat Household

Tagalong and her brother Creamsicle both love the windowsill.

Tagalong isn't the only cat in the Sinkwitz household. In fact, she isn't even the first cat. Before she joined the family, her brother Creamsicle was adopted in July of 2017.

Tagalong and Creamsicle, again in the windowsill.

For their part, Creamsicle and Tagalong actually get along quite well. While not exactly the best of friends, they're certainly friendly. That's not to say their relationship is not without conflict, however.

Tagalong and Creamsicle playing with one another.

There are certainly times that Tagalong and Creamsicle can squabble. Attention is a common one, with Tagalong often jealous when Creamsicle is getting pet.

Tagalong trying to scare Creamsicle away.

Food is another big one. One of the factors that went into feeding the cats at scheduled meal times was Tagalongs frantic rush to get food. With each cat fed separately, they both get a bowl of food to munch on. It hardly stops Tagalong from stealing her brother's food, but it helps a bit.

Tagalong and Creamsicle hanging out on the dining room table with their human brother Joey.

Having two cats is certainly complicated, and there's no guarantee that the cats will get along. Before Tagalong, Creamsicle had another cat sister, Snickers, who he never got along with, because of the dramatic age gap between them. There is no real way to ensure that two cats will be best friends, every cat will be different. Slow introductions are important when it comes to acclimating two cats with each other. If you introduce a new cat to the home as fast as possible, not only will the other cat feel their space has been invaded, but the new cat will also be overwhelmed by their surroundings.
